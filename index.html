A heatmap of your routes, your personal records and every activity in one place. Load your Strava export or any .gpx / .fit files. They're read inside this browser and never uploaded.

Drop your Strava export (.zip or folder) or activity files here

Supports .gpx, .fit, .gpx.gz, .fit.gz and Strava's activities.csv

Get your data from Strava

  1. On strava.com open Settings → My Account.
  2. Under Download or Delete Your Account, choose Get started → Request your archive.
  3. You'll get an email with a .zip (it can take a few hours). Drop that zip here as is. No need to unzip.

Garmin, Wahoo, Coros and others: export .fit or .gpx files and drop them in.

Your data stays yours

  • Files are processed by this page in your browser. There is no server and no upload.
  • The page's security policy blocks it from sending data anywhere. Only map tiles and the page's code are downloaded.
  • From a Strava export only activities.csv and the activity files are read. Profile, followers, messages and photos are ignored.
  • Results are remembered in this browser only. Clear data wipes them.
